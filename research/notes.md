# Source notes

- Source date: 2026-09-18
- Public idea: [Player matching that fills local game store tables](https://www.ideabrowser.com/hub/ideas/player-matching-that-fills-local-game-store-tables-4f007804)
- Newsletter framing: “a dating app, for game nights”—solo players state game, skill, and availability, then receive a compatible table and venue.
- Why now: renewed board-game participation; mahjong and chess clubs are visible social formats; local stores already have tables but often lack reliable player formation.
- Comparable signal cited by the source: StartPlaying raised $6.5M after matching people into online tabletop role-playing games. That is directional evidence for paid matching, not proof of local-store demand.

The public idea page redirected to sign-in when fetched through a browser, so no private scoring, keyword, offer-ladder, pricing, revenue, difficulty, or GTM fields were copied. Those metrics are therefore marked **unavailable**, not inferred. The product plan uses explicit hypotheses that must be validated.

This repository intentionally excludes the raw newsletter, recipient metadata, tracking links, and unsubscribe data.
